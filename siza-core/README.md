# Siza Shared Card Core

Shared card infrastructure used by the Card Generator and `siza-mobile-test`.

- `card-effects.js` defines and validates structured effect descriptors and owns generic effect-presence, amount-aggregation, self/opponent side resolution, `bounce-other-permanent` target enumeration, preferred-owner target selection, pure bounce planning, counter-stack target-index selection, the pure runtime effect plan for counter/draw/damage/observe/bounce/discard execution, the pure priority-response card selector used by rival priority, the pure rival Main-phase card selector (exclude Lands and reactive counters, require a payable plan, prefer lower printed difficulty), the pure priority-window plan (`responder`, `active`, `phase`), the pure priority-pass transition to Stack resolution, the pure post-Stack completion plan for game-over, pending-choice, player return, or rival return, the pure Stack-continuation predicate that preserves the current 30-resolution safety limit plus pending-choice/game-over stops, and the pure match-winner selector from player/rival life totals. The core resolves eligible/query/plan results; Arena's `applyCardEffectV1()` is now an executor that applies the returned plans while Arena still owns actual Stack/graveyard/hand/life mutation, pending-choice state, damage FX, logs, `checkWin()`, bounce execution, rival Equipment precedence, `openManifest()`, `resolveStackAll()` iteration, `resolveTopStack()`, Adventure victory progression, battlefield/UI rebasing, priority-window mutation, persistence, rendering, timers, and presentation.
- `card-schema.js` normalizes card data and preserves effect metadata across handoff, including `equipCost`; it also owns the runtime-equivalent Equipment classification, Equipment-cost queries, and pure Stack resolution classification (`creature`, `artifact`, `equipment`, `spell`). Arena still owns the actual zone insertion, effect execution, logs, and graveyard mutation.
- `cards.js` is the official shared card catalog.
- `card-renderer.js` renders both standard Generator cards and the Mobile/Arena surface.
- `card-renderer.css` styles the standard Generator surface.
- `manifest-rules.js` owns the pure affinity, difficulty, natural-chance, Manafestation requirement, roll-deficit, eligible manifest-bonus-source calculations, the rival roll plan that decides whether the current first bonus source is used, which hand indices become Mana Burn, or whether the roll is unrecoverable, the pure player Burn-selection planner that adds/removes eligible Reserve indices without exceeding the current deficit, the pure Burn-consumption plan that sorts selected indices descending and rebases the manifesting card index step by step, the pure Manafestation success/outcome calculation, the pure Stack-entry plan (`cardId`, owner, reactive counter target), and the pure failure-continuation plan. Arena still resolves card types through `cardById`, applies/exhausts chosen bonus sources, assigns the returned Burn selection, performs the actual hand `splice()` / exile mutation from the returned consumption order, performs Ofrenda sacrifice, creates/pushes the actual Stack object id, writes logs, opens priority, schedules rival continuation, persists/renders, and preserves its historical `aiFailure`, `burnSelected`, and failure-total semantics.
- `crystal-rules.js` owns printed-pip requirements, spell crystal cost, direct-payment planning, the pure Ofrenda/payment planner, crystal-pool refresh, and atomic crystal spending. Arena injects `cardById` into the planner so generated/test cards remain resolvable; turn sequencing, Ofrenda sacrifice, and other match-state decisions remain local to Arena.
- `entry-rules.js` owns the three experimental creature-entry modes plus pure preparation, attack eligibility, available-attacker, and legal-blocker calculations. Both player and rival attacker selection now reuse this shared eligibility calculation; Arena injects the active entry mode, `cardById`, and shared spell-cost calculation while exhaustion, battlefield mutation, trigger application, and combat resolution remain local.
- `creature-rules.js` owns creature counters, equipped-card lookup, descriptor-driven Equipment power bonuses, effective power/toughness, aligned battlefield add/remove mutations, pure `attack-declared` damage aggregation, the pure combat outcome plan, the pure player blocker reassignment plan, and the pure AI blocker assignment plan. Arena applies those plans to the existing match-state objects while life, graveyards, logs, win state, turn state, FX, toasts, and UI orchestration remain outside the shared core.

Arena now resolves the supported card behavior from structured card data for `resolve`, `enter`, `attack-declared`, `combat-damage`, `manifest-roll`, and `equipped` events. This covers the current stack/entry effects, Contrabandista Carmesí, Ignimite, Prisma de Enfoque, and Espada de Bajamar. Permanent regressions also exercise generated cards with unknown IDs to ensure these behaviors are driven by descriptors rather than catalog-specific conditionals.

The current Equipment runtime intentionally supports `Equipar {1}` only. The schema rejects larger `equipCost` values until multi-crystal Equipment payment has its own explicit runtime contract and tests.

The original Arena monolith was canonicalized to remove its historical shadow declarations for combat, priority, battlefield removal, and rendering. Current CI also inlines shared/integration modules for JSDOM, so its aggregate declaration scanner may report repeated helper names across module scopes; those are not the old monolith shadow declarations.
