Case study

Keep story progression inside player control.

An independently developed vertical slice connecting exploration, environmental response, dialogue and objective state.

Problem

Separate features needed one playable flow.

First-person movement, interactions, dialogue choices, objective messages and scene progression had to feel like one investigation rather than disconnected prototype scenes.

Constraints

Every state change had to remain readable.

The slice needed to return control cleanly after staged sequences and explain doors, discovered items, blocked paths and new objectives without breaking immersion.

Decision

Connect phases through explicit feedback.

Exploration, interaction and dialogue advance a shared investigation flow. Camera control, player control and objective presentation hand off deliberately between playable and staged moments.

Contribution

Solo vertical-slice development.

I assembled and programmed the playable flow, first-person control, interactions, dialogue presentation, objective feedback, scene progression and technical integration.

Verified result

The vertical slice works as one continuous case.

The trailer and focused captures show player-controlled exploration, readable environmental changes, selectable dialogue and objective-driven progression running in Unity.
