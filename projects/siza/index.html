Siza

A role-playing systems project combining a deterministic persistent World Engine, a visual MUD-style adventure layer, grounded AI narration and a card-based combat system. The core idea is action and consequence: player and NPC actions become persistent state, knowledge, social reactions and new behavior instead of disposable story text.

Project architecture

Action, consequence and persistent context.

Siza is structured as a systems project rather than a single isolated prototype.

Adventure layer

Persistent visual MUD exploration.

Locations, NPCs, objects, knowledge and consequences live as persistent state. The AI can interpret or render context, but deterministic systems remain authoritative.

Combat layer

Card-driven encounters.

The TCG is a separate tactical authority for full combat encounters. A future Combat Bridge will send encounters from the World Engine and return structured outcomes for persistent consequences.

World layer

Worldbook-grounded simulation.

Rivarica defines canon; the World Engine converts that canon into Rooms, actors, actions, Knowledge, institutions and consequences.

Design goal

Action and consequence.

An action is not finished when prose appears on screen. It is finished when its result becomes persistent world state that other systems can discover, remember, transmit and act on.

Current result

A connected simulation core ready for content.

The World Engine now supports persistent actions and consequences, four resolution families, Knowledge/Facts with lifecycle, grounded dialogue, NPC goals and movement, social Fact transfer, faction authority and institutional reporting policies. The next production focus is content, TCG completion and integration rather than endlessly expanding the core.

System relationship

World → action → rules → consequence.

  1. 01Worldbook

    Defines canonical setting and authored context.

  2. 02World Engine

    Maintains persistent state, actors, knowledge, institutions and legal actions.

  3. 03Resolution

    Deterministic checks resolve uncertainty; full combat is delegated to the TCG when the Combat Bridge is integrated.

  4. 04Grounded AI

    AI interprets or narrates only authorized context and never owns persistent mutation.

  5. 05Persistence

    Consequences become the next world state and can generate Facts, goals and further reactions.
